Project 11: Smart Traffic Navigation System

Problem Statement
Urban traffic congestion has become a significant challenge due to the rapid increase in the
number of vehicles. Drivers often experience delays because of traffic signals, road
construction, accidents and peak-hour congestion. Conventional navigation systems generally
recommend the shortest geographical distance but may not always provide the fastest travel
route considering current traffic conditions. The objective of this project is to develop an AI-
based Smart Traffic Navigation System that recommends the optimal driving route by
considering both travel distance and estimated traffic conditions. The road network is
represented as a weighted graph where intersections are nodes and roads are weighted edges
representing travel time or congestion level. The intelligent agent analyzes multiple possible
routes and selects the most efficient one. The project demonstrates how heuristic search
algorithms improve decision-making in transportation systems.

Objectives

 Model road networks as weighted graphs.
 Consider traffic conditions.
 Recommend the fastest route.
 Display total travel cost.
 Recalculate routes when congestion changes.
